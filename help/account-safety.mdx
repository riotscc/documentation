Keep your profile and account secure.

## Troubleshooting steps

Identify the exact issue and try the simplest likely causes first.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Riots.cc">Go to [Riots.cc](https://riots.cc) and sign in if the feature requires an account.</Step>
  <Step title="Find the relevant section">Look for the feature or setting described on this page. Its label may vary as the product evolves.</Step>
  <Step title="Review and confirm">Check the available options, make the appropriate changes, and verify the result.</Step>
</Steps>

## Good to know

If the problem persists, document what happened and request assistance.
