## Request help

You can use the community [Discord](https://discord.gg/riots) to look for assistance. Confirm that the invitation is current before sharing it publicly.

When asking for help, include the page you were on, what you expected, what happened, and any non-sensitive error text.

<Warning>Never send passwords, authentication tokens, or private recovery codes to anyone offering support.</Warning>
