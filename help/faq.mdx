## General

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What is Riots.cc?">Riots.cc is a place to bring your profile, socials, and projects together under one shareable link.</Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I get started?">Start with the [Quickstart](/quickstart) guide and create your profile.</Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I personalize my page?">Explore the [Appearance](/customize/appearance) documentation for customization topics.</Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Where can I get help?">Start with [Troubleshooting](/help/troubleshooting) or visit [Contact support](/help/contact).</Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Your account

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How do I protect my account?">Use a unique password if password login is available, protect connected accounts, and never share sign-in codes.</Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What should I check before sharing my profile?">Preview your public page and remove anything you do not want visitors to see.</Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
