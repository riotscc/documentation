## What to include

A clear report makes issues easier to reproduce.

- **Where:** Page URL or dashboard section
- **What happened:** Actual behavior or error text
- **Expected result:** What you wanted to happen
- **Steps:** Actions needed to reproduce the issue
- **Environment:** Browser, device, and approximate time

## Share the report

Use the available support channel listed in [Contact support](/help/contact). Remove private account information from screenshots and logs.
