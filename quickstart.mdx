Get started in four steps.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">Visit [Riots.cc](https://riots.cc) and begin registration.</Step>
  <Step title="Choose your username">Pick a memorable name for your public profile.</Step>
  <Step title="Personalize your page">Open the dashboard and add profile details and links.</Step>
  <Step title="Review and share">Preview your page, check its public information, and share your profile URL.</Step>
</Steps>
